About Me

A non-traditional path into safety — and that's exactly what makes me different.

I started my career with a Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) from Indira Gandhi National Tribal University, which gave me strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and a process-driven mindset.

But I realised my real passion was in protecting people on the job — making sure workers go home safe every single day. So I pursued a Post Graduate Diploma in Fire Technology and Industrial Safety, earned my NEBOSH IGC, and built a career in HSE.

Today I deliver safety training, conduct hazard assessments, manage Permit to Work systems, and help organisations build a real safety culture — combining the discipline of my technical background with hands-on field experience at DRDO, solar energy sites, and international events.
