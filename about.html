About Me

A technical foundation. A practical approach to safety.

Based in Abu Dhabi, UAE, I work as an HSE Instructor at Talent Training Institute L.L.C, bringing together technical thinking, field experience and workplace learning.

I hold a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) from Indira Gandhi National Tribal University, with an examination year of 2024. My technical education developed my analytical thinking, attention to detail, and approach to documentation.

My fire safety experience began in March 2023 at Miracles Enterprises, before I completed my BCA. I then undertook a PG programme in Fire Technology & Industrial Safety from July 2024 to June 2025 and received my NEBOSH IGC on 17 March 2025.

My experience includes an apprenticeship in the Safety Engineering Division at HEMRL, DRDO, from 3 December 2024 to 3 June 2025, rooftop solar safety work, and HSE instruction at Talent Training Institute L.L.C. I am also a Highfield registered and approved tutor, registered on 21 August 2026, with an approved training scope documented on 25 September 2026.

How my background shapes my work

Computer applications gave me a foundation in analysis and structured documentation. Fire safety work and my DRDO apprenticeship added practical experience with inspections, risk assessment and emergency preparedness. HSE instruction brings these areas together through clear explanations and workplace examples.
