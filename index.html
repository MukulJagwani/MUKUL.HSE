01 / ABOUT ME

A technical foundation.
A practical approach to safety.

I’m Mukul, an HSE Instructor based in Abu Dhabi. My journey brings together computer applications, fire safety and workplace learning.

My fire safety experience began at Miracles Enterprises in March 2023, while I was pursuing my Bachelor of Computer Applications at IGNTU. I completed my BCA examination in 2024, developing an analytical approach to problem-solving and documentation.

I went on to complete a PG programme in Fire Technology & Industrial Safety from July 2024 to June 2025, and was awarded the NEBOSH International General Certificate on 17 March 2025.

During my apprenticeship in the Safety Engineering Division at HEMRL, DRDO, from 3 December 2024 to 3 June 2025, I gained experience in safety audits, risk assessment and fire drills. My work also includes rooftop solar safety and HSE instruction at Talent Training Institute L.L.C.
